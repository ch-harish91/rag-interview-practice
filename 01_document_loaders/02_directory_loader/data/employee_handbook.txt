ABC Technologies Employee Handbook

Welcome to ABC Technologies. We are committed to providing a professional, respectful, and collaborative work environment for all employees.

Working Hours
- Office Timing: 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM
- Working Days: Monday to Friday
- Lunch Break: 1 Hour

Code of Conduct
- Maintain professional behavior at all times.
- Respect colleagues and company policies.
- Protect confidential company information.
- Follow all security guidelines.

Attendance
- Employees should report to work on time.
- Late arrivals should be communicated to the reporting manager.
- Repeated absenteeism may result in disciplinary action.

Work From Home
- Employees may work from home with manager approval.
- Availability during office hours is mandatory.

Performance Review
- Performance reviews are conducted every six months.
- Promotions and salary revisions are based on performance.

This handbook is applicable to all employees of ABC Technologies.
