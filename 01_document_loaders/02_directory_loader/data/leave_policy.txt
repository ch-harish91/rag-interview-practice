ABC Technologies Leave Policy

Employees are entitled to the following leave benefits:

1. Casual Leave: 12 days per year
2. Sick Leave: 10 days per year
3. Earned Leave: 15 days per year
4. Maternity Leave: As per company policy
5. Paternity Leave: 7 days

Leave Application Rules

- Employees must apply for leave through the HR portal.
- Leave requests should be submitted at least 2 days in advance.
- Emergency leave should be reported to the reporting manager immediately.
- Unused earned leave can be carried forward according to company policy.

This leave policy is effective from January 1, 2026.
